# SmartMerce Pitch

## 15-Second Pitch

SmartMerce is an AI commerce agent that shops for you without giving AI control of your money. The AI discovers, compares and recommends products. Deterministic policy, user authorization and wallet signing control the spend.

## 30-Second Pitch

AI agents are getting good at deciding what we should buy, but letting probabilistic software freely spend from a wallet is unsafe. SmartMerce solves this by separating intelligence from financial authority. The AI finds the best product, while deterministic infrastructure verifies budget, merchant, price, stock, network and authorization before payment can happen.

## 60-Second Pitch

SmartMerce is an AI commerce agent for safe autonomous shopping. A user can say, "Find me the best wireless earbuds under 6 XSGD," and SmartMerce parses the request, searches a trusted merchant catalogue, compares products and recommends the best option with clear reasons.

The important part is what happens next: the AI still cannot spend. A deterministic policy engine checks transaction limits, daily limits, merchant allowlisting, stock, server-side price and the user's stated budget. Then the user must authorize an exact product, merchant and amount. Payment infrastructure is prepared for XSGD on Avalanche C-Chain through a non-custodial wallet, and checkout creates merchant orders only after verification.

SmartMerce is built around one principle: AI should decide what to buy, not whether it is allowed to spend your money.
