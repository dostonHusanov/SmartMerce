# SmartMerce 60-Second Demo Script

Target: 55 to 60 seconds.

## 0-7 sec

AI can already tell us what to buy. But letting an AI freely spend our money is dangerous.

## 7-13 sec

SmartMerce separates intelligence from financial authority. The AI recommends actions. Deterministic systems authorize money movement.

## 13-20 sec

Here I enter: "Find me the best wireless earbuds under 6 XSGD. Prioritize rating and value."

## 20-30 sec

SmartMerce understands the request, detects the budget, searches trusted merchant products and compares price, rating, reviews, shipping and value.

## 30-38 sec

It recommends Soundcore Mini Buds because they have the strongest balance of price, rating and review confidence within the budget.

## 38-45 sec

But SmartMerce still cannot spend. The deterministic policy engine checks transaction limit, daily limit, merchant allowlist, product availability, server-side price and the shopper's budget.

## 45-52 sec

Only after user authorization does SmartMerce prepare payment. The authorization is scoped to the exact product, merchant and amount for five minutes.

## 52-57 sec

With official XSGD and merchant wallet configuration, payment is executed through the user's non-custodial wallet on Avalanche C-Chain Mainnet. The merchant console only confirms orders after verification.

## 57-60 sec

SmartMerce. AI that shops. You stay in control.

## Backup line if payment configuration is not entered

For this judging environment, the payment rail is intentionally blocked until the official XSGD contract and trusted merchant wallet are configured. That is a safety feature, not a mock.
